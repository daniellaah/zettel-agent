# Rotating held-out folds in cross-validation

K-fold cross-validation randomly divides observations into approximately equal groups. Each group becomes the validation set once, while the method is fit on the remaining groups. The fold errors are averaged to estimate test error. The book identifies leave-one-out validation as the case with one fold per observation and describes five or ten folds as common choices that reduce the number of model fits.
