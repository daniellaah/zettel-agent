# Sample-ratio mismatch in experiment monitoring

Sample-ratio mismatch occurs when observed treatment and control user counts do not satisfy the expected allocation ratio. The article describes this as a problem that typically invalidates the experiment and makes its other metric movements untrustworthy. It recommends checking for mismatch early and setting alerts. Feature teams can then investigate the cause and stop an experiment when its results cannot be salvaged.
