# Stable segments for experiment analysis

The article recommends segmenting by characteristics unlikely to change because of treatment, such as market or activity measured before the experiment. Segmenting by activity during treatment can move users between segment values as a consequence of the change being tested. It recommends checking segment stability and allocation balance. For dynamic behavioral segments, it points to weighted metrics rather than treating the segment membership as fixed.
