# Date segmentation and novelty effects in experiments

The article examines treatment effects separately by date to identify changes hidden by an overall average. Its example of a changed mail button shows an initially large click increase that declines over subsequent days. It interprets this pattern as evidence of a novelty effect requiring investigation. Single-day spikes may instead involve data-quality issues or unusual events, and may not generalize beyond the experiment period.
