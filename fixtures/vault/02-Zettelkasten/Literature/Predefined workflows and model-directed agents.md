# Predefined workflows and model-directed agents

The article distinguishes workflows, whose orchestration follows predefined code paths, from agents that dynamically direct their process and tool use. It recommends starting with the simplest adequate design and adding complexity when outcomes justify it. Workflows suit decomposable, well-defined tasks, while agents offer flexibility for open-ended paths. The article identifies increased latency, cost, and compounding errors as considerations for autonomous systems.
