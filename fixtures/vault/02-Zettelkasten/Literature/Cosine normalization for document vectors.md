# Cosine normalization for document vectors

The vector-space model represents documents with term-weight components and loses the relative ordering of terms. Cosine similarity divides the vector inner product by the product of Euclidean vector lengths. This compensates for differences in vector magnitude arising from document length. The book also expresses cosine as the inner product of unit-normalized document vectors and applies the same representation to query-document scoring.
