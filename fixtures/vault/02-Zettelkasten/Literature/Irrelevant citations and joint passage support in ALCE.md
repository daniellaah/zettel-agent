# Irrelevant citations and joint passage support in ALCE

ALCE's citation precision identifies citations that do not contribute support to a generated statement. Its procedure considers whether a cited passage supports the statement alone or whether removing it leaves the other cited passages sufficient. This accommodates statements requiring combined evidence from several passages. The paper presents precision alongside citation recall so that a fully cited answer can still be assessed for unnecessary or irrelevant references.
