# Attention sharpness experiments in MIND

The MIND experiments vary the power in label-aware attention from zero to infinity. At zero, every interest receives the same attention and the representation becomes an average without reference to the target item. The authors report worse performance for this setting, improving results as the power increases, and faster convergence and the best performance under hard attention in their comparison.
