# Rank-based combination with reciprocal rank fusion

Reciprocal rank fusion sums a reciprocal contribution from each system's rank for a document, using an added constant to moderate the influence of very high positions. The method combines rankings without requiring comparable raw retrieval scores or training examples. The authors fix the constant at 60 after a pilot investigation and retain it for subsequent validation. Their reported improvements concern the particular TREC and LETOR combinations studied.
