# Simple models and independent infrastructure tests

The guide recommends starting with a simple model while establishing reliable training examples, meaningful targets, and serving integration. Simple features make it easier to verify that training and serving receive the intended values. It also recommends testing data ingestion and model export independently of learning, including comparing scores from the training and serving environments for a fixed model and the same example.
