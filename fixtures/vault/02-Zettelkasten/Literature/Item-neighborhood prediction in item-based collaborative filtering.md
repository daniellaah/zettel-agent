# Item-neighborhood prediction in item-based collaborative filtering

The item-based algorithm selects items rated by the target user that are most similar to the item being predicted. It computes their similarities and combines the user's ratings on these neighbors. In the weighted-sum approach, each rating receives its item-similarity weight, and the sum is normalized by the absolute similarity weights. Thus the prediction depends on both an item neighborhood and the active user's ratings within that neighborhood.
