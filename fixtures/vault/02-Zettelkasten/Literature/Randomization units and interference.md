# Randomization units and interference

The article identifies identifier stability, network effects, and enterprise constraints as considerations when choosing randomization units. Cookie churn can move users between variants and complicate long-term estimates. Features affecting collaborators can violate independence assumptions between users. Enterprise requirements may prevent user-level assignment altogether. The article discusses cluster or enterprise-level randomization for such settings and warns against using a single identifier type for every experiment.
