# Logistic probabilities and linear log odds

Logistic regression applies the logistic function to a linear predictor to produce probabilities between zero and one. The book shows that the logarithm of probability divided by one minus probability is linear in the input. A one-unit predictor increase adds its coefficient to the log odds and multiplies the odds by the exponential of that coefficient. Its effect on probability depends on the current predictor value.
