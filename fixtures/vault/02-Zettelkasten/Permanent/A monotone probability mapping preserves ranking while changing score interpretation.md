# A monotone probability mapping preserves ranking while changing score interpretation

For a binary logistic model, applying the sigmoid preserves the ordering of finite logits. A ranking can therefore remain unchanged while its scores acquire a probabilistic interpretation. Ranking quality alone cannot establish that the probabilities match observed frequencies; the ordering and the numerical probability scale answer different questions.
