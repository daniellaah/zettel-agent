# Optimization success and generalization success are different outcomes

Reducing the training objective shows that the optimizer is fitting the supplied examples more successfully. It does not establish lower error on the future population. Model capacity and the relationship between observed and future data affect that second outcome. A training curve therefore needs an independent generalization measurement before it supports a claim that the predictor improved in use.
