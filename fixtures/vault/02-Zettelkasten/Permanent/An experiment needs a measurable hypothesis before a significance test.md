# An experiment needs a measurable hypothesis before a significance test

A statistical test can assess movement in a defined metric, but it cannot repair a hypothesis whose intended effect is never measured. Declaring success metrics before examining outcomes connects the product claim to observable evidence. Guardrails and data-quality checks add different information: they assess unacceptable side effects and whether the observations can support the comparison at all.
