# Verifiable rewards depend on the task and checker boundary

An exact answer checker or a code test suite supplies objective feedback under a defined task contract. Its coverage still limits what success means: passing available tests does not establish correctness for every possible input or every unstated requirement. Reward design benefits from verifiability while remaining dependent on the quality of the verifier and the scope of the task.
