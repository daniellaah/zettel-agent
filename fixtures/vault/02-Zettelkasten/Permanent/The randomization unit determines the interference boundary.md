# The randomization unit determines the interference boundary

User-level assignment assumes that one user receiving treatment does not materially change another user's outcome through the tested feature. Collaboration features can violate that assumption. Choosing a cluster or enterprise unit changes both the assignment boundary and the estimand. The right unit follows the interaction structure and product constraints, rather than simply whichever identifier is easiest to hash.
