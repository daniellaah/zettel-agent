# A coefficient association does not establish a treatment effect

A fitted logistic coefficient describes an association within the model and observed data. It does not by itself specify the outcome of intervening on that feature. An intervention can change other variables or the data-generating process, so interpreting a coefficient as causal uplift requires additional identification assumptions or experimental evidence.
