# A baseline comparison makes model promotion a relative decision

An absolute quality threshold can accept a model that is materially worse than the one already serving. Comparing the candidate with a baseline adds the question of whether this replacement is acceptable now. The threshold, baseline, and evaluated data must remain visible in interpreting a promotion decision.
