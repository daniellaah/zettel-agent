# Retry success and reliable first delivery answer different product questions

A task solved once after many attempts may demonstrate capability while offering poor reliability for a user expecting one correct delivery. At-least-one-success and all-trials-success metrics expose this difference. Comparisons need an explicit retry budget and selection rule. Otherwise additional attempts can make a system appear better while hiding the cost and inconsistency experienced in ordinary use.
