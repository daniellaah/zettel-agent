# An aggregate uplift can conceal a transient novelty effect

A treatment may produce an early reaction that fades during the experiment. Averaging over the entire period can hide that trajectory and encourage an overly durable interpretation. Date-level inspection reveals whether the effect persists or depends on particular days. Such patterns are evidence for investigation, not by themselves a complete explanation of why user behavior changed.
