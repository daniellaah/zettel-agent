# Sleep consolidates what you practiced

A night of sleep after practice is not dead time for learning. Memories formed during the day appear to be replayed and reorganized while asleep, and performance on both facts and motor skills is often better the next morning than it was at the end of the practice session, with no further practice in between.

Two consequences for how I schedule study. First, splitting a hard topic across two evenings with a night in between beats one long session of the same total length. Second, pulling an all-nighter before an exam trades the consolidation of everything learned that week for a few extra hours of shallow review — a bad deal.

The mechanism is still debated, and the effect sizes in lab studies vary a lot. I treat it as a reason to protect sleep, not as a precise rule.
