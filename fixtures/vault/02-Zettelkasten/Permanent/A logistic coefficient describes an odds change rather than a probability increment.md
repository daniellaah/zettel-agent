# A logistic coefficient describes an odds change rather than a probability increment

A constant change in log odds produces different probability changes near the center and the extremes of the logistic curve. Reporting a coefficient as a fixed percentage-point uplift misstates the model. An explanation of its probability effect needs a specified starting probability or feature configuration, with other predictors held fixed.
