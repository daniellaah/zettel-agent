# A readable trajectory is diagnostic evidence rather than a correctness proof

A recorded trajectory helps locate failed searches, mistaken interpretations, or inappropriate actions. Its readability does not establish that the final answer is correct or that every stated rationale explains the model's internal computation. Traces support diagnosis alongside outcome checks. Treating them as a complete proof would confuse an observable interaction record with independent evidence that the task was accomplished.
