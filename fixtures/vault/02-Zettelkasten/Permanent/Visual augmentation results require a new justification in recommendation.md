# Visual augmentation results require a new justification in recommendation

Evidence that cropping and color distortion help image representations does not specify which behavior-sequence transformations preserve user intent. A recommendation adaptation needs its own positive-pair definition and checks for information lost by that transformation. The transferable idea is learning agreement under justified transformations; the visual operations themselves do not supply that justification.
