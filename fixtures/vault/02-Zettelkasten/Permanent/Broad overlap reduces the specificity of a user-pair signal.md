# Broad overlap reduces the specificity of a user-pair signal

Two users who share many clicked items provide less specific evidence for any one item pair than users whose overlap is narrow. Swing represents that distinction by reducing each contribution as shared history grows. The score consequently depends on the surrounding behavior graph, not just the number of users who clicked the two target items.
