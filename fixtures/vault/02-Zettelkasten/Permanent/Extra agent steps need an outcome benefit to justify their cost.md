# Extra agent steps need an outcome benefit to justify their cost

More model calls and tool decisions consume time and resources and introduce additional failure opportunities. Their value should be assessed through task outcomes under an explicit budget. A flexible loop may be justified for unpredictable research paths while a fixed workflow suffices elsewhere. Architecture complexity is consequently a hypothesis about improved performance, not evidence of improvement by itself.
