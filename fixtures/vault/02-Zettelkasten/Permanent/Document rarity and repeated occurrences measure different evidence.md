# Document rarity and repeated occurrences measure different evidence

A term can occur many times inside a few documents while being rare across the collection. Within-document frequency measures repetition; document frequency measures how widely the term is distributed. Combining them distinguishes concentrated evidence from ubiquitous vocabulary. This explains why total corpus occurrence count alone cannot substitute for document frequency in a term-discrimination argument.
