# R1-Zero and R1 represent different training interventions

The direct-RL experiment and the later multi-stage model answer different questions about how reasoning behavior is developed and made usable. Cold-start supervision, rejection-sampled examples, and broader alignment alter the intervention. Evidence about R1-Zero should therefore not be described as though the final R1 model had received no supervised post-training. Model lineage matters when interpreting a capability claim.
