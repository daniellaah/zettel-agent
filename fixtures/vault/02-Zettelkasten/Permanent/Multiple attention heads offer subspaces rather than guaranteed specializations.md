# Multiple attention heads offer subspaces rather than guaranteed specializations

Different learned projections allow attention heads to attend in different representation subspaces. That architectural opportunity does not guarantee that each head acquires a distinct human-interpretable role. Semantic specialization is an empirical interpretation requiring inspection or intervention. This parallels [[A learned interest slot does not supply its own semantic label]]: multiple latent components provide capacity without automatically supplying names for their functions.
