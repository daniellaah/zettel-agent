# Frequent monitoring needs a corresponding inference procedure

Repeatedly inspecting a conventional fixed-horizon significance test and stopping at a favorable result changes its error behavior. Monitoring remains useful for operational regressions, but inferential stopping needs an appropriate sequential or multiple-testing procedure. The schedule for looking at metrics and the rule for concluding an effect should be designed together rather than treating extra observations as statistically free.
