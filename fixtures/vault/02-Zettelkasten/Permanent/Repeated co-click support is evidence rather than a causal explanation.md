# Repeated co-click support is evidence rather than a causal explanation

Multiple users clicking two items provides stronger structural support than one isolated connection under the Swing design. It still reflects observed behavior, which may be influenced by exposure, popularity, or shared circumstances. A higher graph score is therefore an association used for recommendation. It does not show that either item caused demand for the other.
