# Citation presence is weaker than citation support

A citation marker shows that an answer refers to a source, but only comparing the claim with that source establishes whether the reference supports it. Correctness and support can also diverge: a true statement may cite the wrong passage. Evaluating these dimensions separately makes grounded-answer quality more precise and prevents a high citation count from substituting for evidence verification.
