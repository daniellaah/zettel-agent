# Post-treatment segments can change the population being compared

If treatment changes engagement, grouping users by their observed engagement during the experiment can move them into different segments in the two variants. Within-segment comparisons may then mix treatment effects with selection effects. Pre-experiment activity offers a more stable segmentation variable. This is why a segment label needs a timing definition, not merely a familiar behavioral name.
