# Compaction is a lossy state transition that needs continuity checks

A summary replaces part of the previous task state and can omit constraints whose importance emerges later. Continuing successfully therefore depends on preserving decisions, unresolved issues, and evidence needed for the next steps. Compaction quality should be judged by continuity of task behavior, not just compression ratio or readability. Persistent notes can supplement the summary when precise details must remain recoverable.
