# Factor dimension controls both sharing and expressiveness

A small latent dimension constrains interaction coefficients to share a compact representation. Increasing dimension permits a richer pattern of pairwise relationships while adding parameters. Under sparse observations, added capacity may exceed the evidence available for estimation. Factor dimension is therefore a statistical capacity choice as well as a memory and computation setting.
