# Sample-ratio mismatch challenges the comparison before the outcome

Unexpected treatment-control counts suggest a problem with assignment, exposure, or observation. A favorable outcome metric does not resolve that issue because the affected population may already be selected differently. The allocation check is logically prior to interpreting treatment effects. Investigating mismatch protects the validity of the comparison instead of merely adding another metric to a dashboard.
